# 10,000 People. Apparently, I’m a Community Now 🚀✨

**Author:** Olga Kryuchkova  
*Nordic Tech and Innovation Ecosystem Explorer | ICT Trainer | Certified AI Transformation Leader | MSc (Tech.) | Engineer*  
**Published on:** August 27, 2026

---

10,000 subscribers. Is that a small number or a big one?

Perhaps the more interesting question is: 10,000 relevant people — is that enough to start something meaningful?

I think it is.

Numbers are seductive. They make us count, compare, optimize, celebrate, and occasionally forget that behind every number there is a human being, with their own experience, curiosity, expertise, ambitions, and wonderfully unpredictable connections.

So I’m not particularly interested in having 10,000 subscribers.

I’m interested in having 10,000 people who are relevant to the conversation:

*   People working on ideas.
*   People building companies.
*   People shaping ecosystems.
*   People asking uncomfortable questions.
*   People who know something I don’t.
*   And, hopefully, people who occasionally think: *“Hmm. I hadn’t looked at it that way before.”*

That feels like a much more interesting kind of milestone.

So yes — 10,000 is a big number.

But it is also a wonderfully small number when you think about how many people are out there. Which makes it, perhaps, the perfect number to begin with.

Thank you for being here. 💓

Now, let’s make the number count for something. 🥂 And, let’s see what happens at 100,000. 😉
